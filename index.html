Our Vision

The Strategic Roadmap for Team Triveni Sangam

What We Are Doing

Standardizing CI/CD pipelines, containerizing multi-tier applications, and leveraging Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform) across hybrid cloud environments to guarantee reproducible builds.

What We Want to Achieve

Achieving zero-downtime deployments, full automated telemetry coverage with centralized logging, automated rollback workflows, and reducing deployment cycle times to under 3 minutes.

Expanding into MLOps automation pipelines, GitOps continuous delivery using ArgoCD, serverless container scaling via Azure Container Apps, and automated DevSecOps vulnerability scanning.
